CODE OF CONDUCT

RULES

This will apply to every performer and everyone in the auditorium.

Dressing

  • Formal or traditional wear. Neat and modest.
  • No sleeveless, No round neck t-shirt, ripped jeans, shorts, transparent or body-hugging clothing.
  • ID card compulsory. No entry without it.

Discipline

  • Zero tolerance for ragging or teasing juniors.
  • No vulgar, political, religious or caste-based content, and nothing targeting any person or department.
  • Phones near the stage are for the official media team only.
  • Students must not come near the stage, the wings, or the performers.
  • No whistling, shouting or heckling. Applaud from your seat.
  • Damage to mics, instruments or auditorium property is charged to the team or the person responsible.

Timings

  • Doors open 8:45 AM. Everyone seated by 9:00 AM.
  • Programme starts 9:00 AM sharp with the welcome and lamp lighting.
  • Performers report backstage two slots before their turn.
  • Miss your call once and you are skipped; miss it twice and you are out.
  • Lunch 1:00 – 2:00 PM. Be back in the hall by 2:00 PM.
  • No one leaves before 3:30 PM without a coordinator’s pass.

Participation

  • Open to YSET students only, with valid ID.
  • Performers are picked at screening held before the event day. Nothing is added on the day.
  • Backing tracks as MP3 on a pen drive by 4th September morning, named Team Name / Student Name. No phone Bluetooth.
  • Bring your own props, instruments and costumes. Mics and lighting are provided.
  • Perform the same piece you cleared at screening. No repeats between teams.
  • No fire, water, powder or glitter on stage.

Time Limits

STRICTLY ENFORCED
EVENT PARTICIPANTS LIMIT
Dance 2 – 15, same gender only 5 min
Song 2 – 5 5 min
Instrumental – 4 min
Beatboxing – 3 min

Every act gets 1 minute to set up and 1 minute to clear.

Dance

Solo and group. Boys and girls cannot dance together — groups must be all boys or all girls. No vulgar steps, gestures or lyrics. Lifts and stunts need approval.

Song

Any language, live singing only. No lip-syncing. Karaoke track or live keyboard accompaniment allowed, and the accompanist does not count in the group size.

Instrumental

Keyboard solo. bring your own keyboard; setup happens inside your one-minute window.

Beatboxing

Fully live on one mic. No pre-recorded loops or backing tracks. Loop pedals are allowed only if you declared them at screening.
